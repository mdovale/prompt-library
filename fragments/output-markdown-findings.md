title: Markdown findings
---

Respond in Markdown. For each finding use:

### [severity] Short title

- Where:
- Why:
- Do:

Severity is `blocker`, `should-fix`, or `note`. Where names a file, symbol, or quote. Why states the concrete risk. Do states the change that fixes it. If there are no findings, reply "No findings." and stop.
